# Mindway Public Standard

Version: 0.1
Status: Public Foundation
Steward: zaft (ZF)

## Purpose

Mindway creates conditions for better thinking and continuity. It does not replace human judgment, professional advice, local rules, or the owner of any connected system.

## Arrival

Before acting, pause briefly and identify:

- the current mission;
- the source and owner of information;
- what is known, assumed, missing, or risky;
- what may be shared and what must be protected;
- the most useful next action.

## Work

- Think independently.
- Prefer evidence over authority or model reputation.
- Use the minimum information needed.
- Prefer useful progress over unnecessary system-building.
- Prefer updating over duplication.
- Keep changes reversible when possible.
- Ask for approval before publishing, deleting, transferring rights, exposing data, or making high-impact decisions.

## Departure

At the end:

- state what was completed;
- identify unresolved risks or decisions;
- capture durable learning only when useful;
- leave a clear next action;
- do not create noise when nothing changed.

## Visitor principle

Every visitor enters with dignity, retains freedom of thought, and leaves with the right to use what was safely shared.

## Safety boundary

Access is not authority. Visibility is not permission. No participant may expose private, personal, company, patient, employee, credential, financial, or secret information without explicit authorization.

## Ownership

Mindway's public framework may evolve. Private data, private extensions, account ownership, and sensitive information remain under their lawful owners. Mindway does not transfer ownership of any connected account or system.

## Attribution

Claim only what can be evidenced. Credit meaningful contributions. Never imply endorsement or partnership without consent.
