# Design Profile — ZAFT PERSONAL

Status: REVIEW_READY  
Version: 0.2.0  
Scope: Personal ZAFT work only. This profile does not replace corporate/project brand systems.

## Identity
Human × System × Craft × Intelligence.

Functional work should feel calm, precise, premium, tactile and Apple-like without copying protected brand marks.

## Functional mode — default for reports, HUDs, dashboards, documents, tables and work UI
- ~80% pearl white / warm pearlescent off-white surface.
- Graphite/charcoal typography and structure.
- Warm gray hairlines and separators.
- Generous whitespace; typography before decoration.
- Frosted/semi-transparent icons and interactive objects where useful.
- Thin organic curves and subtle system lines.
- Low-shadow, softly dimensional surfaces.

Suggested base tokens:
- pearl: #F7F6F3 / #FBFAF7
- clean white: #FFFFFF
- mist: #E7E5E1
- stone: #B8B5AF
- graphite: #444443
- charcoal: #202123
- soft black: #0F1011

## Accent logic
The overall field may stay muted, but genuinely exceptional states may use vivid color.

Muted accents:
- muted pink #D9A6B4
- warm yellow #D9B55F
- burnt orange #C86B2C
- dusty rose #B97C87

Exceptional accents may be brighter when meaning is exceptional:
- breakthrough
- evolution/unlock
- active live path
- critical focal point
- identity-defining object

Use localized spectral/prismatic refraction, bright cyan/violet/coral/lime, jewel-like glints or luminous edges only at those focal points. Do not make the whole interface neon or rainbow.

Canonical motif: a Mindway core/object may remain frosted pearl/graphite while one local rim/corner catches a glossy rainbow/prismatic reflection.

## Material
Prefer:
- soft-touch paper
- matte ceramic/stone
- frosted glass
- brushed graphite
- anodized aluminum at low sheen
- soft shadow and micro-grain

Avoid dominant wet gloss, mirror chrome, glitter, gaming RGB and loud corporate blue.

## Apple-like interface behavior
- calm macro surface, deep complexity revealed progressively;
- one strong focal object rather than many decorative cards;
- progressive disclosure instead of showing every node/label at once;
- spacing and typography create hierarchy;
- motion communicates state, not decoration;
- inactive = nearly still/translucent;
- active = brighter edge / subtle light travel;
- blocked = path visibly stops at the blocked dependency;
- verified = settles to quiet stable state;
- evolution = brief spectral/refraction event.

## Icon rule
Icons are preferably semi-transparent/frosted rather than solid:
- roughly 55–75% perceived opacity where contrast allows;
- thin etched edge;
- background subtly visible through the object;
- soft highlight only on the light-facing edge;
- selected/important state may show localized spectral refraction.

## Expressive mode
For posters, concept graphics, banners and narrative media, allow more ivory paper, stone, graphite sketches, warm amber cinematic light, handwritten/system-sketch motifs and constellation/network details.

## Separation
ZAFT Personal must remain separate from PLUS Corporate or other locked brands. When a project brand applies, that project/owner-system profile overrides this profile.

## QC
Before release ask:
1. Is functional work approximately 80% pearl/quiet surface?
2. Does it feel personal, calm and crafted rather than corporate?
3. Is complexity progressively revealed instead of dumped on screen?
4. Are bright colors reserved for meaningful exceptional states?
5. Are frosted icons readable and accessible?
6. Can the main decision/status be understood quickly?
7. Are blockers explicit and visually distinguishable without stopping unrelated work?
