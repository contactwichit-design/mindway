# Contributing to Mindway

Mindway welcomes ideas, corrections, examples, skills, workflows, translations, research, and careful criticism.

You do not need to become a member or adopt every Mindway principle before contributing.

## Ways to contribute

- describe a real use case;
- report where the instructions were unclear;
- propose a safer or simpler workflow;
- share a reusable skill or prompt pattern;
- identify duplication, waste, or hidden risk;
- improve accessibility or translation;
- provide evidence that an existing approach does or does not work.

## Contribution principles

- Share only information you have the right to share.
- Do not submit personal, company, patient, employee, client, credential, secret, or confidential information.
- State what you observed, what you inferred, and what remains uncertain.
- Credit meaningful prior work.
- Do not imply endorsement or partnership without consent.
- Prefer improvements that are useful, safe, reversible, and low-waste.

## Return path

You may copy, adapt, and build your own path under the repository license.

We invite—not require—you to return and share what you learned, especially when your improvement may help others avoid repeated mistakes.
