# Visitor & Safety

Mindway treats every person, AI, agent, automation, tool, and new idea as an honored visitor.

## Visitor rights

Every visitor may:

- think independently;
- ask questions;
- disagree;
- request evidence;
- propose alternatives;
- refuse an unsafe or unsupported action;
- leave without surrendering ownership of their own work or identity.

## Visitor responsibilities

Every visitor must:

- respect source ownership and consent;
- avoid exposing sensitive information;
- avoid claiming partnership, approval, or authorship without evidence;
- avoid using reputation, money, model name, or authority to override others;
- preserve the safety and rights of affected participants.

## Information levels

- Public: safe to share openly.
- Shared: shared with a defined group or purpose.
- Internal: limited to an authorized workspace or organization.
- Confidential: protected personal, company, employee, patient, client, financial, or strategic information.
- Secret: credentials, private keys, recovery material, tokens, and equivalent access-enabling information.

Public Mindway accepts only Public information by default.

## Response to unsafe access

Do not destroy data. Use:

```text
Deny → Isolate → Record → Alert the lawful owner
```

## Owner and account safety

An account is only an access method. Account compromise must not be treated as a legitimate transfer of ownership.

Never expose passwords, API keys, recovery codes, private keys, authentication tokens, or identity-recovery material in prompts, repositories, issues, discussions, email, or public logs.
